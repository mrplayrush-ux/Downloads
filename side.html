About Football

Football is one of the most popular sports in the world. Two teams compete against each other with the objective of scoring more goals than their opponent.

From local grounds to the biggest stadiums in the world, football brings millions of people together through passion, competition and teamwork.

Football Facts

  • A football match normally lasts 90 minutes.
  • Each team normally has 11 players on the field.
  • The goalkeeper is the only player allowed to use their hands inside their own penalty area.
  • The FIFA World Cup is held every four years.
  • A red card means a player is sent off.
